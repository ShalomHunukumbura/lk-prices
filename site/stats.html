Sri Lanka food prices: full data

A monthly food price index built from every Daily Price Report the Central Bank of Sri Lanka has published since August 2016: about 40 staples in the main markets, wholesale and retail.

 
 
 

Food price index

August 2016 = 100. Each month is compared with the one before on the prices reported in both, with every item counted equally.

CBSL didn't publish fish or fruit prices from early 2019 to July 2020, so this basket has a gap.

About these numbers

  • Source: the Daily Price Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka. The prices are CBSL's; the index, medians and margins are computed here. This project is not affiliated with CBSL.
  • About ten years of PDFs in three layouts are read by position and checked: each report's "yesterday" column is compared with the previous report, and 99.7% agree. A handful of typing slips (a price that jumps tenfold and comes straight back) are left out.
  • From 2021 to 2025 the index is within 10% of a direct comparison of average prices for every basket. Wholesale fruit before 2020 rests on three items in one market, so read it loosely.
  • USD prices use the Central Bank's average indicative rate for the month.
