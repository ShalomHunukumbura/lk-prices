Prices come from the Daily Price Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka. The index, monthly medians and markups are worked out here from those reports. This site is not affiliated with the Central Bank.

Item prices are monthly medians at Pettah where it reports them (Negombo for fish). The index counts every item equally and compares each month with the one before. US dollar prices use the Central Bank's average rate for the month.
